BUTTER CHICKEN

Description

Butter chicken is classic Indian dish made with marinated & grilled chicken, simmered in a creamy tomato gravy/sauce. The sauce is super silky, buttery, aromatic and mildly spicy.

This lip-smacking and delicious dish is hugely popular among not only the Indian peaple but also among the tourist form all over the world who come to India.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thigh fillets, sliced
  • steamed Basmati rice and or naan to serve
  • dried fenugreek leaves and coriander leaves, for garnish

Merinade Ingredients

  • 1 thumb size piece fresh ginger, finely grated
  • 6 cloves garlic, finely grated
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1½ tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
  • 120ml greek yoghurt

Sauce Ingredients

  • ½ stick/55 g unsalted butter
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 Tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 Tbsp grated garlic
  • 1 Tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp garam masala homemade or store-bought
  • 1 green chilli such as jalapeno, serrano, or Bird’s eye, minced
  • ¾ cup/180 g tomato paste
  • 2 Tbsp chopped cilantro, tender stems and leaves
  • fine sea salt
  • 1 cup/240 ml heavy cream

Steps

  1. If using bone-in chicken thighs, make 2 to 3 deep gashes in the chicken; skip this if you use the boneless pieces.
  2. Place all the ingredients for the marinade in a medium bowl or ziptop bag and combine until smooth. Fold in the chicken and make sure the chicken is completely coated with the marinade. Leave in the refrigerator covered for 1 hour, preferably overnight.
  3. When ready to cook, preheat the oven to 400F/200C.
  4. Lay the marinated chicken pieces out on a baking dish or roasting pan.Roast the chicken in the oven until the internal temperature reaches 164F/74C, 30 to 45 minutes (boneless chicken takes about 20 minutes), rotating the dish halfway through during cooking.
  5. Transfer the baking dish to the upper rack of the oven, and then broil over high for 2 to 4 minutes, until the chicken starts to char in a few spots. Remove the baking dish from the ove
  6. At the 20-minute mark of cooking the chicken, start the sauce. Melt the butter in a medium Dutch oven or saucepan over low heat. Once the butter begins to foam, stir in the oil. Add the Kashmiri chilli powder, toasted cumin, garlic, ginger, garam masala, and green chilli.
  7. Stir until fragrant, 30 to 45 seconds. Then add the tomato paste and cook until the paste begins to turn light brown, 5 to 6 minutes. Pour in the heavy cream and add the chicken and any liquids left behind in the baking dish. Fold to coat well. Stir in the lemon juice, taste, and season with salt
  8. Bring to a boil over medium-high; the fat should separate from the sauce. Remove from the heat. Garnish with cilantro and salted butter if using. Serve hot or warm with rice or naan.
